## Full description

```text
🔴 X Unfollow Radar helps you identify accounts that appear not to follow you back and manage your X/Twitter Following list with confidence.

✨ KEY FEATURES

🔍 Automatically scans and scrolls through the Following page
👤 Identifies accounts without a visible “Follows you” label
⭐ Protects selected accounts with a whitelist
🛡️ Skips accounts that match your keyword filters
👁️ Preview mode lists detected accounts without unfollowing anyone
🚀 Normal mode automatically unfollows eligible accounts
⏱️ Uses random 2–5 second delays between actions
📋 Separately reports real, previewed, skipped, and failed results
📊 Displays a 30-day statistics chart for real actions
🌍 Supports English, Turkish, and German interfaces
🌙 Includes light and dark themes

📦 PLANS AND LIMITS

🆓 Free: Up to 50 real actions within a rolling 24-hour window
💎 Pro: Up to 500 real actions within a rolling 24-hour window, plus CSV export
👁️ Preview actions do not consume your real-action quota
⏳ Each real action leaves the safety counter 24 hours after it occurs

🔐 PRIVACY

🏠 X usernames, filters, whitelist entries, preferences, and action history are stored locally in Chrome extension storage.

🚫 The extension does not use analytics, advertising, telemetry, or a developer-operated tracking server.

🔑 Only the Pro license key is sent directly to Gumroad when license verification is required. X usernames, Following lists, filters, history, page content, and X credentials are not sent to Gumroad.

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Detection relies on the visible “Follows you” information shown by X. If X changes its interface, the detection logic may require an update.

👁️ Use Preview mode to review detected accounts before enabling real actions.

Controlled pacing does not guarantee that X will not restrict an account. Use the extension responsibly and at your own risk.
```
